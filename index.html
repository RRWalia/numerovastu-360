    Frequently asked questions

    Is NumeroVastu 360 really free?

    Yes. The full numerology and Vastu engine — Lo Shu grids, Driver and Conductor, name and mobile analysis, dasha timeline and remedies — is free to use with no account, no subscription and no report limit.

    How do I install it on Android or iPhone?

    On Android, open the site in Chrome and tap the “Install app” button in the header (or Chrome’s menu → Install app). On iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. It then launches full-screen from your home screen like any other app.

    Is my personal data stored anywhere?

    No. All calculations run inside your browser on your device. Saved charts are kept in local storage on that device only and are never uploaded to any server.

    What is a Lo Shu grid and what do missing numbers mean?

    The Lo Shu grid is a 3×3 square that places the digits of your birth date into fixed positions. Repeated digits show reinforced traits, while missing digits point to areas that need conscious support — the app explains each one and suggests practical remedies.

    What is the difference between Driver and Conductor numbers?

    The Driver (Mulank) comes from your date of birth and describes your natural working style. The Conductor (Bhagyank) comes from the full date and describes the life path and results you move toward. Their compatibility is a core part of Ank Jyotish.

    Does the app work offline?

    Yes. After the first visit the app and its knowledge pack are cached on your device, so you can generate complete reports with no internet connection.
